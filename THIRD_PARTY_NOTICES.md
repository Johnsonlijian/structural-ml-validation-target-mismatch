# Third-party notices

This package redistributes no third-party data. It contains project-authored scripts, aggregate
result tables computed from public datasets, and figures generated from those tables. Datasets remain
under their own licences; consult the `licence` column of `DATASETS_AND_LINKS.csv` before reusing the
underlying measurements. Entries marked `TO VERIFY` are listed with their official identifier so the
licence can be confirmed from the publisher page; nothing in this package depends on that
confirmation, because no third-party rows are redistributed.

Two identifiers recorded in the project's own reproduction metadata were found not to resolve during
this work and were corrected (shear-wall study DOI; DesignSafe coupling-beam database). Both
corrections are quality control of this project's metadata and are reported as such, not as evidence
about the external datasets.
