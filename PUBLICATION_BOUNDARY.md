
# Publication and release boundary

Status: **public software and aggregate-evidence release authorized on
2026-08-02**.

This repository may publish the source, protocol projection, frozen environment
metadata, aggregate summaries, public-data pointers and rights records listed
in its cryptographic receipts.  It must not contain raw third-party data,
replicate-level outputs, partial aggregates, an active manuscript, submission
correspondence, internal workflow history, credentials or private records.

Third-party datasets remain at their official versioned records.  The package
links to them and records their licences; it does not redistribute raw or
row-level material.  Aggregate headed-stud derivatives are segregated under
`app/` by their CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 source partitions.  The MIT
licence for project-authored code and documentation does not override those
data-specific terms.

Public release authorization does not assert article submission, acceptance or
publication.  Formal journal submission remains outside this repository and
outside the scope of the release authorization.
