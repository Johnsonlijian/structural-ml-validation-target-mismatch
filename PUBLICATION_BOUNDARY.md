# Publication boundary

- Supports a manuscript under review; not to be posted as a preprint before the journal's policy
  allows it.
- No third-party rows are redistributed; datasets are obtained from the identifiers in
  `DATASETS_AND_LINKS.csv`.
- No DOI has been minted. `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json` carry placeholders to complete if a DOI
  is assigned later.
- The repository tag cited in the manuscript's code-availability statement must match the tag of this
  release once the author pushes it.
